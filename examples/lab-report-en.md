# Cooling a beaker of water

Course context: a fictional first-year lab, PHYS 199. The numbers below were chosen for this example.

## Method

We poured 200 ml of tap water into a glass beaker and heated it until the water reached 60 C. The beaker then stood on the open bench. We recorded the temperature every 30 seconds for three minutes. We did not stir the water, and we did not cover the beaker.

## Data

At 0 s the temperature was 60 C. At 30 s it was 57 C. At 60 s it was 55 C. At 90 s it was 53 C. At 120 s it was 51 C. At 150 s it was 50 C. At 180 s it was 49 C.

## Comment

The temperature fell quickly at first and then more slowly. However, we never measured the room temperature, so we cannot say how close the water came to the room. Therefore the note only describes the table. We did not fit a cooling model, and we do not claim a physical law from seven readings. A later group could repeat the timing and also record the room, which would make the table easier to discuss.
